# Commercial Video

Live-action product shots, ads, brand films and UGC. Software, apps, services, and brands whose own look is UI, type or illustration use `code-built-video.md` instead. Write prompts with `prompt-craft.md`.

## Selling Points Become Actions

- Every claim is one visible action, never an adjective: "hydrating" → droplets condensing and sliding down the bottle; "premium" → a named light (rim light angle, color temperature, shadow softness); "durable" → the material under stress, recovering.
- A product never jumps from packaged to in use: start it ready to use, or give opening it its own beat.
- A physical process gets at least three visible stages, each an unfinished mid-action state: a pump of foundation pooled on the cheekbone; one fingertip tapping its center, not yet spread; slow circles that have spread it halfway, the edge still visible.
- The product stays recognizable and accurate in every shot: its reference in every segment, its proportions in words, its logo never redrawn by the model (set it in code).

## Product Shot and Brand Film

- Product alone: centered and stable, macro close-up with one slow move, never handheld; the last 2 seconds for the logo when the brand wants one.
- Brand film: person and product together, neither dominating; cinematic micro-moments (a boot crushing wet grass, a jacket catching wind) instead of feature callouts; one specific environment and light; no dialogue unless the brief has it.
- With a reference ad, take its camera, pacing and expressions as words, not its people or content.

## UGC and Presenters

It must feel like a real person sharing a find, not an actor reading a script.

| Avoid (commercial) | Target (UGC) |
|---|---|
| Perfect skin, studio light, locked camera | Natural skin, window or ring light, handheld selfie with slight sway |
| Smooth scripted claims ("lasts all day") | Discovery in real time ("It's been like six hours. Nothing.") |
| Only benefits | One honest flaw ("比我预期的稀一点"), which makes the rest believable |

**Open on one hook (first 3 seconds):** the problem state the product fixes; a viewer comment answered to camera ("你们让我测，我来了"); or a skeptic who starts using it at once.

**Prove each claim with its demo:**

| Claim | Demo |
|---|---|
| Texture | Touch, stretch, scrunch or pour it; show how it behaves |
| Coverage, color | Apply to one side only, the bare side in frame |
| Wear, durability | A timestamp, then the condition after the time |
| Fit | Wear it and move: sit, spin, raise arms, walk away |
| Craft | Extreme close-up, fingers along a seam, zip or finish |
| Before / after | Both in one continuous shot, no cut |
| First use | The first reaction: surprise, hesitation, discovery |

Keep unscripted micro-reactions ("等等，这个…"), third-party proof (a watch, a scale, a tape measure), one or two non-promoted items in frame, and a casual presenter and home setting. Dialogue lines read as noticing, not announcing: if a line could appear in a brand ad, rewrite it.

**Talking-head segments:** when a segment is mostly the presenter talking, needs one exact or cloned voice, or runs longer than a clip, make the voice track first and animate the portrait with the digital-human model (`omnihuman-2.0` in model-routing). Keep generated video for the segments where hands, products and camera move.
